Our approach

Make us your 1st choice for loans.

1st Choice Loans presents itself as a Winchester office serving Franklin County and nearby areas. The business focus is straightforward: personal loans from $100 to $1,000, a simple application process and local personal management.

Borrowers often contact the office after a bill, repair or income problem creates pressure. Some are trying to move away from payday advances or title loans. Others need a defined payment schedule after a medical expense, job change or household emergency. The office conversation is designed to make the request understandable before a borrower signs loan documents.
